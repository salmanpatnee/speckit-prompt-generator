## The Strategy: 3 Pillars

### Pillar 1: GitHub Excellence (Foundation)
**Goal:** Create an attractive, professional repository that welcomes contributions

**Key Elements:**
- Clean repository structure
- Comprehensive documentation
- MIT License (permissive, encourages adoption)
- Clear contribution guidelines
- Responsive issue/discussion management

**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks setup, then ongoing

**Effort:** Medium (mainly documentation)

**Result:** 100-500 stars in first month, foundation for community

---

### Pillar 2: LinkedIn Visibility (Reach)
**Goal:** Build awareness among target audience (SDD practitioners, Claude users)

**Key Elements:**
- Launch announcement (strong engagement driver)
- 3-4 posts per week (consistent visibility)
- Share insights about SDD & prompt engineering
- Engage with community comments (build relationships)
- Long-form articles (thought leadership)

**Timeline:** Ongoing content, starting immediately

**Effort:** Low (1-2 hours per week)

**Result:** 1000+ views per week, community awareness, relationship building

---

### Pillar 3: Community Engagement (Growth)
**Goal:** Build active community that drives adoption and improvements

**Key Elements:**
- Respond quickly to issues/discussions
- Feature user projects and testimonials
- Accept and celebrate contributions
- Regular updates and improvements
- Transparent roadmap

**Timeline:** Start at launch, sustain indefinitely

**Effort:** Medium (5-10 hours per week initially)

**Result:** 10-50+ active community members, user-generated content
