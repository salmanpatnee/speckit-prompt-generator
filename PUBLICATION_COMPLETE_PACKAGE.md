## ❓ FAQ: Publication Strategy

### Q: Should I wait for more features before launching?
**A:** No. v1.0.0 is perfect for launch. Users want to get involved early. You can improve based on feedback.

### Q: How do I handle negative feedback?
**A:** Listen, learn, and improve. Negative feedback is often more valuable than positive. Show you're responsive and care about quality.

### Q: When should I reach out to influencers?
**A:** After you've published and have initial traction (50+ stars, some discussions). Personalized messages > generic outreach.

### Q: Can I switch platforms later?
**A:** GitHub is permanent. LinkedIn presence can adapt. Start with what you have.

### Q: What if growth is slower than expected?
**A:** That's normal. Focus on quality over vanity metrics. One engaged community member beats 100 passive followers.

### Q: Should I monetize this?
**A:** Not immediately. Build community first. Monetization options emerge naturally (sponsorships, premium features, services).
